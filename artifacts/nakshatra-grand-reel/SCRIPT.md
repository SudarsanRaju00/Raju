# Nakshatra Grand — Evening Table

## Director's treatment

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, designed for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok.
- **Promise:** Make a viewer feel that Nakshatra Grand is the place to go when dinner should feel like an occasion.
- **Identity:** Deep charcoal, warm ivory, burgundy, earthy red, and muted gold. Editorial serif display type paired with a clean sans-serif and a Telugu-compatible serif. The brand name appears plainly and respectfully; no fabricated logo asset is introduced.
- **Hero:** A banana-leaf thali and the food itself, treated as the center of gravity. The existing food photography carries the film while typography and celestial linework give it a signature.
- **Motion:** Macro push-throughs, circular apertures, split reveals, and typography that resolves in staggered beats. Fine orbit lines and textile-like rules remain restrained and anchored to the subject.
- **Arc:** Hook the viewer with a close, tactile food detail; open into the restaurant's point of view; move through the generosity of the meal; then settle into a clear brand lockup and visit details.
- **Payoff:** The ending inverts the palette to a warm ivory field and lets the name, Telugu line, location, and hours breathe in a centered lockup before the loop returns through a burgundy circular seal.
- **Runtime:** 27 seconds total. Six scenes: 4.2s + 4.4s + 4.6s + 4.5s + 4.8s + 4.5s.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–04.20s / 4.20s — “COME HUNGRY.”

**Purpose:** Make the first second tactile and appetizing, then establish the restaurant's confident voice.

**Exact content:** `A TABLE FOR THE EVENING` at 00.18s; `COME` at 00.55s; `hungry.` at 01.05s; `LEAVE` at 02.00s; `glowing.` at 02.36s. Small Telugu line `రుచితో నిండిన ఒక అందమైన సాయంత్రం` at 03.02s. Keep the final food phrase readable through 03.58s, then begin the handoff.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Start with the hero thali photo cropped so the rice, fried garnish, banana leaf, and dark curry occupy most of the frame. A fine burgundy circle sits over the plate like a celestial eclipse; it is the aperture carrier for the film. Type lives in the upper half so the food owns the lower half.

**Timed choreography:** 00.00–00.35s: photo begins at 1.15 scale with a slight warm blur and settles toward 1.03. 00.12–00.55s: the gold eyebrow and rule draw from the left. 00.55–01.00s: `COME` snaps in with stiff scale and a short blur-to-sharp settle. 01.05–01.55s: `hungry.` rises into place with a cream-to-gold color shift. 01.70–02.20s: a radial burgundy ring traces around the thali while `LEAVE` appears on the opposite rhythm. 02.36–02.92s: `glowing.` arrives with a slight italic tilt, then resolves. 03.02–03.55s: the Telugu line fades in; a subtle orbit tick rotates 12 degrees. 03.58–04.20s: the circle scales toward the lens, the photo softens, and the ring becomes a full-frame gold curve.

**Transition contract:** The closing burgundy ring is the same geometry as the opening ring of Shot 02. It scales through the lens while the outgoing type shears up and the next burgundy field is already visible underneath.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `public/images/hero-thali.jpg` copied from the website artifact. Framer Motion scale, blur, clip-path circle, masked text, and SVG-like CSS orbital rules. No fabricated logo.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental bed begins with a restrained hand-drum pulse and a soft room tone. No voiceover. Planned first type hits at musical accents; alignment remains unverified until the bed exists.

### Shot 02 / 04.20–08.60s / 4.40s — “A LITTLE MORE THAN DINNER.”

**Assets and technique:** No photo. CSS panels, masked typography, perspective unfold, fine textile-like rule marks. Use the bundled Google Fonts and a Telugu-compatible serif.

**Sound intent:** Add a soft wooden click at the panel unfold only if the generated instrumental bed contains a natural accent; do not synthesize a separate SFX track.

### Shot 03 / 08.60–13.20s / 4.60s — “ANDHRA, SERVED.”

**Transition contract:** The final thali crop is the same subject used in Shot 04, opening slightly soft and oversized before settling sharp at center.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `public/images/gongura-mutton.jpg`, `public/images/dosa-table.jpg`, and `public/images/hero-thali.jpg`. Use image planes with clip-path windows, scale-through, and a cream split band.

**Sound intent:** Let the instrumental bed pick up slightly here. No claims of exact beat sync until the audio is generated and checked.

**Assets and technique:** Existing `public/images/hero-thali.jpg`; circular aperture, anchored orbit ring, light sweep, separate shadow layer, masked type.

**Sound intent:** The bed should briefly breathe here, leaving room for the viewer to read the hero line.

### Shot 06 / 22.50–27.00s / 4.50s — “NAKSHATRA GRAND.”

**Exact content:** `NAKSHATRA` at 23.04s; `GRAND RESTAURANT` at 23.52s; `నక్షత్ర గ్రాండ్ రెస్టారెంట్` at 24.22s; `Come hungry. Leave glowing.` at 25.15s; `SITARAMPURAM, ANDHRA PRADESH` at 26.06s.

**Timed choreography:** 22.50–23.10s: star motif draws from the collapsed rule, four strokes arriving one after another. 23.04–23.80s: `NAKSHATRA` resolves from wide tracking to a tight, composed wordmark. 23.52–24.05s: `GRAND RESTAURANT` appears beneath in small caps. 24.22–24.78s: Telugu name fades in with a muted gold baseline rule. 25.15–25.75s: final promise rises into place, centered. 26.06–26.58s: location settles at the bottom safe area. 26.58–27.00s: the star motif scales 1.15 toward camera and becomes the opening burgundy aperture; the loop begins without a dead tail.

**Transition contract:** The star/aperture is the same circular carrier that opens Shot 01. Its final expansion should overlap the first food image by one frame, creating a continuous loop rather than a hard reset.

**Assets and technique:** Typography and deterministic SVG/CSS star linework. No logo asset is fabricated; the plain wordmark is the brand treatment.

**Sound intent:** Let the instrumental bed resolve with a restrained final note and carry the loop on the same tonal center. Beat alignment must be verified after audio exists.

## Variety review

- Shot 01 is a close, food-led macro with an aperture and large stacked type.
- Shot 02 is a graphic split/unfold with no photography.
- Shot 03 is a fast multi-image crop sequence with hard typographic beats.
- Shot 04 is a centered, slower hero reveal with an anchored ring.
- Shot 05 is an editorial information field with measured data choreography.
- Shot 06 is a quiet centered end card and loop carrier.
- Adjacent shots vary scale, field, composition, dominant mechanic, and pacing. Every shot has a post-entrance event and an outgoing handoff. The final 15–20% of each shot is reserved for a visible transition, never a blank hold.
